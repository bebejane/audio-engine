Tape Delay — license and attribution
====================================

The tape character in this folder (source.js) is a JavaScript port of the
re-deemer tape-emulation DSP:

  * wow + flutter + scrape flutter capstan model
  * Jiles-Atherton tape hysteresis (RK4), after Chowdhury DAFx-19
  * record/repro emphasis, head bump, gap loss, spacing loss
  * tape types and an "age" macro

  from https://github.com/naturarum/re-deemer (crates/te2-dsp/src/tape/*)
  Copyright (c) 2026 naturarum
  Licensed under the ISC license (reproduced below).

The base delay / feedback / mix structure follows cyrusasfa/TapeDelay
(https://github.com/cyrusasfa/TapeDelay).
